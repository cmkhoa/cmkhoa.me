## Hello

I'm Michael Cao, a Computer Science student at the University of Illinois
Chicago. I build reliable backend systems and AI infrastructure, with a
particular interest in the systems that make LLM-powered products dependable in
practice.

My work spans concurrent storage, local model orchestration, Rust tooling, retrieval systems,
and reproducible LLM evaluation. I'm especially drawn to projects where careful
engineering, measurement, and simple interfaces matter as much as the model.

## What I'm doing

- Building [RedisClone](https://github.com/cmkhoa/redisclone), a sharded
  Redis-compatible server in Go, and [myLlama](https://github.com/cmkhoa/myLlama),
  a localhost control plane for `llama.cpp`.
- Developing [Canvas MCP](https://github.com/cmkhoa/canvas-mcp), a Rust server
  that gives AI clients read-only access to course content, deadlines, and grades.
- Completed the v0.8 [MURL grading study](https://github.com/cmkhoa/uic-murl-llm-grader-finetune)
  with a frozen Qwen3-4B model and a one-time held-out evaluation: 47/47 valid
  outputs, 39/47 correct labels, and first-error exact match on 24/26 eligible examples.
- Practicing algorithms and competitive programming through
  [ICPC preparation](https://github.com/cmkhoa/icpc-prep-2026).
