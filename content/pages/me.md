## Hello

I'm Minh Khoa Cao, a Computer Science student at the University of Illinois
Chicago. I build reliable backend systems and AI infrastructure, with a
particular interest in the systems that make LLM-powered products dependable in
practice.

My work spans concurrent storage, local model orchestration, retrieval systems,
and reproducible LLM evaluation. I'm especially drawn to projects where careful
engineering, measurement, and simple interfaces matter as much as the model.

## What I'm doing

- Building systems projects in Go and Python, including a Redis-compatible
  server and a local `llama.cpp` runner manager.
- Conducting undergraduate research on LLM-assisted grading of worked calculus
  solutions at UIC.
- Actively competing in ICPC and preparing for the Mid-Central USA regional.

## Beyond engineering
